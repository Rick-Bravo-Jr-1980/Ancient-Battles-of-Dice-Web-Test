## Current playable content
The browser package now follows player access: tutorials and Act I/II battles, plus skirmish armies from those two acts. Skirmish opponents follow the same restriction. Later Macedonian, Imperial, Thracian and Indian development content is not included merely because its art is approved. It remains saved in the development library.

The current battles, including Lechaeum, retain their artwork, animation frames and narration. No blanket downscaling or frame reduction was used. Existing progression gates remain; Act II skirmish armies become available after Plataea. Developer profiles no longer bypass the army scope.

## Playing and feedback
Open the tutorial or your unlocked campaign battle. Complete the tutorial to begin skirmish progression. Please report missing portraits, unavailable controls, or any mismatch between the selected and computer armies. Existing saves are retained; a later-army save cannot be resumed in this scoped browser build, but remains available in the full local development project.

## Validation
Scope, save/resume and campaign progression checks passed. The packed game resolves all 127 current roster options; a missing Helot animation reference was corrected. Eight Chrome/Edge/Firefox fixture runs passed, alongside diagnostics privacy and download checks. On the tested integrated-graphics laptop, Chrome median phase averages ranged from 12.3 FPS in combat to 23.3 FPS during deployment, with long-tail stalls. Browser changes make speed comparisons with the previous release inconclusive. These are automated desktop checks, not a full player playthrough; mobile performance and full rules-table review remain follow-up work. Detailed measurements are recorded in the source repository performance report.
