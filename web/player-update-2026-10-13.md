# 2D Portrait Game Player Update

October 13, 2026 weekly release. The public game receives this update after the release checks and GitHub Pages deployment finish.

This test update brings the approved Thracian peltast and Persian archer animations into their army lists and adds the latest Lechaeum battlefield and campaign progression changes.

## Army portraits and movement

II/12 Alexandrian Macedonian Thracian peltasts and II/7 Later Achaemenid Persian archers now use their approved walking, attack, recoil, falling and fallen artwork in the local game. Playback respects the individual frame durations, including the pause before release and recovery after an attack. These are visual animations; they do not give Ps elements an additional shooting ability.

II/12 Thessalian cavalry and II/7 Persian cavalry now use the approved animation sets. Their corrected walking cycles articulate the horses' knees and hooves, with steady saddle alignment, alongside attack, recoil and casualty poses. These apply to regular Cv elements; generals, Knights and Light Horse keep their own artwork.

The integrated infantry sets also cover Kardakes, phalangites, Hypaspists, Takabara, mercenary hoplites, Agrianian psiloi and Persian subject skirmishers. Cavalry now has dedicated charge cycles. First-pass light-horse action cycles are included for II/12 Prodromoi and II/7 javelin light horse; Saka/archer alternatives keep their separate artwork.

## Lechaeum battlefield

The Athenian test army has seven Ps and five 4Sp elements, including its general. The Spartan army has nine 4Sp, including its general, and three Cv. These are game elements, not claims about historical troop numbers.

Spartan hoplites deploy in three rows of three near the northern settlement. Their three cavalry elements deploy abreast to the west. The arrangement gives the Athenian player time to deploy and makes the Spartans adjust their formation as threats develop.

The gentle hill has been removed. Two difficult hills occupy the revised western and southeastern positions, with woods in the southwest. The road runs near the eastern edge of the built-up area. Test whether these obstacles encourage cavalry to maneuver while leaving useful routes through the battlefield.

Xenophon's briefing introduces the engagement without historical troop totals. Its narration is a dramatic adaptation, not a quotation. The Windows test uses the Mark preview voice; browser narration depends on the voices available on the player's device. Captions remain available.

## Campaign and Psiloi rules

Completing Sphacteria unlocks Delium. Completing Coronea unlocks Lechaeum. Failed objectives and replay results do not incorrectly unlock the next battle; developer access remains available for testing.

The shared Ps outcome handling now applies the appropriate flee result when doubled, including against Spears, subject to the existing opponent, terrain, flank and rear exceptions. A normal flee turns the element and moves it a full flee distance without spending a PIP. Obstructions and battlefield edges still affect the result. Being doubled is not a universal guarantee that Ps will survive.

## Campaign battles and resuming play

Nemea and Coronea now have playable campaign setups and recorded Xenophon briefings. Coronea includes its finished roster, corrected peltast art and alignment, and approved Benedict narration.

Battles now save after settled actions, with one Resume Battle slot per profile. Use Main Menu to leave safely; starting a new battle asks before replacing an unfinished one. Browser saves belong to that browser and device. Closing a tab abruptly restores the most recent completed checkpoint, and clearing site data removes local saves. Browser save persistence across restarts still needs player testing.

## What to test

1. Select II/7 and II/12 armies and check the new cavalry, archer and Thracian artwork during movement, combat, recoil and casualties.
2. Open the Lechaeum briefing and launch its test battle. Check the Spartan formation, terrain routes and room for Athenian deployment.
3. Try Ps against Spears, then compare outcomes involving flank or rear contact and obstacles.
4. Check campaign unlocks after Sphacteria and Coronea. Include a failed objective if reporting a progression problem.

When reporting a problem, include the battle or army list, troop type, action taken, expected result, actual result, browser or Windows version, and a screenshot when useful.

## Play instructions and limitations

Open the web game in a current desktop browser, wait for its substantial initial download, then select or create a profile. Click inside the game to enable audio. Use army selection for II/7 and II/12 tests and campaign battle selection for the historical scenarios. Existing campaign unlocks still apply; developer access remains available for testing.

These are work-in-progress painted animations. Short light-horse recoil and fall sequences, pose transitions, weapon continuity and body registration still need player feedback. Army animation coverage is incomplete. Pending review artwork is not routed into gameplay. Browser voices vary by device. The separate isometric strategic-map experiment is not part of this 2D release.
