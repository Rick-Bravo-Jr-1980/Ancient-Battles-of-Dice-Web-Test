## Validation and limitations

The release passed 13 focused Godot checks covering tutorial flow, voice assets, menus, saves, progression, the Thracian roster/animations and 168 portrait routes. Seven desktop Chrome/Edge benchmark runs and one Firefox run completed their battle and save tests. Collector privacy, explicit downloads and interrupted-session handling passed final checks. These automated checks do not replace a full player playthrough or mobile testing.

Performance remains limited on the tested integrated-graphics laptop: Chrome median phase averages ranged from about 12 FPS in combat to 25 FPS in deployment, with noticeable long-tail stalls. Browser-version changes prevent a fair speed comparison with October 21. One initial audio-device error and one collector timing assertion did not recur on isolated rechecks; their causes are unconfirmed. Recommended next work is controlled profiling of combat and first-use stalls, plus feedback from your device. Detailed measurements are retained in the repository's November 10 performance report.
