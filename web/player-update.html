Lesson 3: combined arms

Lesson 3 now concentrates its introductions on light horse, Psiloi, Auxilia and Bows. Spears and cavalry were introduced in earlier lessons. A shared light-foot briefing explains movement through rough and bad going, individual entry into bad going, and the absence of a woods combat penalty. Unit introductions compare movement allowances and close-combat factors with spears.

When an eligible bow target appears, Miltiades explains the shooting factor against mounted troops (4) and foot (2), then directs you to select the Fast Bows. The selection briefing explains range, arc, movement restrictions and blocked terrain. Argive woods guidance emphasizes the slower movement of solid spears. The lesson objective includes engaging all Argive units.

The compact objective papyrus keeps progression controls such as Commit Orders available. The objective checklist appears during narration and collapses during normal play; combat cards retain priority. The panel tries positions clear of units and stays accessible if the screen is crowded.
