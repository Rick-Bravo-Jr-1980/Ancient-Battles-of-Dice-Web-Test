IMPROVEMENTS, REGRESSION CANDIDATES AND ACTIONS
Confirmed improvement: 18.2% smaller than the unfiltered local package while retaining all 168 audited live portrait/vase paths and 51 narration-manifest passages. All 145 audited source-only assets are absent from the normal pack.
No causal historical speed improvement is claimed. Current on/off medians favor diagnostics for deployment and some casualty frame-time measures, but movement p95/p99, camera FPS/p95/p99 and casualty average FPS cross the review threshold. Repeats did not clear all flags. Camera p99 is particularly variable (516 ms on / 78 ms off median); overall gameplay correctness does not clear this timing concern.
Corrective action: profile the synchronous Godot-to-browser reporting bridge and persistence scheduling, then repeat longer movement/camera/casualty phases with matched browser, GPU, power state and cache conditions. Preserve these results and the October 15 baseline. Do not advertise diagnostics as fully performance-validated until the tail-latency flags are resolved.
Dense formations remain slow on Intel UHD 630 (roughly 13–17 FPS during the measured active phases). Test a discrete GPU separately; do not combine hardware baselines. The site has only about 2.2 MB hosting headroom, so every addition needs a fresh size check.

STABILITY AND CORRECTNESS
All 12 current browser battle runs passed, with identical outcome digests and successful isolated save round-trips. Chromium runs recorded zero JavaScript/console errors, zero nonlocal requests and zero confirmed errors/warnings/suspected stalls in collected reports. Firefox functional acceptance passed eight checks including benchmark/save completion. Injected errors and deliberately interrupted tabs in diagnostics tests are not spontaneous game crashes.
Chrome/Edge collector checks and Firefox error capture, privacy filtering, player-controlled export, interruption/normal-exit handling, bounded history and unavailable-storage behavior passed. Diagnostics use a separate local IndexedDB and do not transmit reports. No zero-crash or long-session stability claim is made; Android, endurance and forced OS/OOM-crash behavior are untested.
Native regression checks passed for live assets, mounted/Psiloi outcomes, charge ranges, campaign gates, Lechaeum, save/resume, II/7–II/12 coverage, I/52 animation/ready/facing/light troops, recorded voices and lesson narration/demonstrations. Twenty full scripted lesson scenarios passed (ten each for Lessons 1 and 2). Scripted headless checks are not a substitute for player feedback. Lessons 3 and 4 have not been tested or updated.
