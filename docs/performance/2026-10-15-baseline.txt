UNRESOLVED ISSUES / CORRECTIVE ACTIONS
High-density rendering on Intel UHD 630 is a performance constraint. Profile formation rendering and asset memory before adding more simultaneous detail. Retest on the intended discrete GPU as a separate hardware baseline.
No average FPS loss was observed; the positive difference is not attributed to diagnostics. Deployment p95 and camera p99 cross the review threshold. Their per-run ranges overlap (deployment off 49-54/on 49-56 ms; camera off 84-212/on 85-94 ms), but this short test does not rule out a tail-latency cost. Repeat these phases in the next release gate before declaring full performance acceptance. Functional acceptance passed; performance sign-off remains conditional.
Final production export size and live internet startup must be checked during the weekly release. Keep the site below 1,000,000,000 bytes. Preserve the tested startup shell and split-pack workflow.
One desktop, no Android measurements; no long-session endurance or forced OS/OOM crash proof.
Earlier engine-delta and competing-game runs were exploratory and excluded from the accepted wall-clock baseline.
No game-dev CLI available; these are direct Godot/browser harness measurements with SHA-256 receipts, not certified GPU timing evidence.
